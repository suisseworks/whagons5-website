A maintenance manager starts the day wanting to know how much work there is and how much of it is late. If answering means counting rows in the grid, the answer comes too late. KPI Cards put those numbers on top of every space, right above the task grid, and they update on their own. This guide covers how to add them with a template, how to use them to filter and sort, and what to know before you build your own.

## Try this first

**Three things for your first day with KPI Cards**

1. Apply the Overview Dashboard template to your spaces.
2. Click In Progress to see only the work under way.
3. Drag Overdue to the front if it's the first thing you check.

## 1. Start with a template

### Add the cards from the space

**What to do.** In a space, use Add KPI card. You can also go to KPI card management and use Add Card. Either way, choose Use Template.

**Why it matters.** Templates come with the cards already set up and save them switched on, so they show up in the space as soon as you apply them.

**How.** If you open the builder from a space, Space Scope already has that space filled in. Leave it empty and the cards apply to every space.

### Choose between Overview Dashboard and Workload Monitor

**What to do.** For a full view of the space, use Overview Dashboard. For just the day's workload, use Workload Monitor.

**Why it matters.** Overview Dashboard brings six cards, which are Total Tasks, In Progress, Finished Today, Overdue, Weekly Trend and By Status. Workload Monitor brings three, which are Created Today, By Priority and Completion Rate. Before you apply, you can untick the ones you don't need and rename the rest.

**How.** For a floor team, four cards are plenty. A short strip reads at a glance.

## 2. Work with the cards

### Click to filter the grid

**What to do.** Click In Progress or Finished Today.

**Why it matters.** Those two cards filter the space's grid down to those tasks. A click on another card, like Total Tasks, clears the filter. The other template cards don't filter.

**How.** Use it in the shift meeting. One click on In Progress and you can go over what each person has open.

### Put what you check first on the left

**What to do.** Drag the cards to change their order.

**Why it matters.** The order is saved for you, in that space. Everyone can keep their own without changing anyone else's.

**How.** Put what you look at first on the left. In maintenance that's usually Overdue.

<Video name="kpi-cards-reorder" fig="C" caption="Overdue moves to the front and the rest make room." label="Animation: the Overdue card is dragged from fourth place to first, and the other cards shift over to make room." />

### Hide the strip when it doesn't help

**What to do.** Use the eye button to hide or show the KPIs. To turn them off for a whole space, switch off Show header KPIs in the space's display settings.

**Why it matters.** Some spaces, like one for long projects, say more through the grid than through numbers. On narrow screens, like a phone, the strip hides on its own.

## 3. If you build cards from scratch

### Switch it on after saving

**What to do.** After you save a From Scratch card, go to KPI card management and switch it on.

**Why it matters.** Cards made from scratch save switched off. Until you turn them on, they don't show up in the space.

### Stick to status and date filters

**What to do.** If you need a count of your own, make a task count card with a status filter or an updated-date filter.

**Why it matters.** Today those are the filters that change the number. Assignee, priority and tag filters get saved but don't change the value. A task count card with a status filter also filters the grid when you click it.

**How.** Some card types show as coming soon in the builder. Pick from the ones that are available.

## 4. Who can create cards

Creating and editing cards needs the workspace configuration permission. Admins and owners have it, and you can give it to a custom role. Everyone else sees the cards without being able to edit them.

Past ten cards the strip gets hard to read, and KPI card management stops offering Add Card once you reach that number.

If you don't see Custom KPI Cards in your organization, ask your admin or your Whagons contact to turn it on.

## Your KPI Cards checklist

Go through it before you show the board to the team.

<Video name="kpi-cards-checklist" fig="D" caption="The checklist at a glance." label="Animation: a card with the KPI Cards checklist in four groups. The boxes tick one by one." />

**The cards**

- [ ] You started from a template
- [ ] Each space shows only the cards people use

**Filtering**

- [ ] The team knows In Progress and Finished Today filter the grid
- [ ] Cards made from scratch are switched on

**Order**

- [ ] What's urgent sits on the left
- [ ] Each person adjusts their own order if needed

**Who edits**

- [ ] Only roles with permission create or edit cards
- [ ] The board gets a look in the shift meeting

When the first question of the morning gets answered by looking above the grid, the cards are doing their job.
