A maintenance manager starts the day wanting to know how much work there is and how much of it is late. **Custom KPI Cards** put those numbers on top of every space, right above the task grid, and they update on their own.

## What's new

- **Two ready-made templates.** *Overview Dashboard* brings six cards: Total Tasks, In Progress, Finished Today, Overdue, Weekly Trend and By Status. *Workload Monitor* brings three: Created Today, By Priority and Completion Rate. Untick the ones you don't need and rename the rest before you apply.
- **Click to filter.** In Progress and Finished Today filter the space's grid down to those tasks. A click on Total Tasks brings everything back.
- **Your own order.** Drag the cards to reorder them. The order is saved per person and per space, so everyone keeps their own.
- **Hide when it doesn't help.** The eye button hides or shows the strip, and a space setting turns it off for the whole space. On phones the strip hides on its own.

## Who can set them up

Creating and editing cards needs the workspace configuration permission. Admins and owners have it, and it can be given to a custom role. Everyone else sees the cards without editing them. A space holds up to ten cards.

## Availability

Custom KPI Cards is a powerup. If you don't see it in your organization, ask your admin or your Whagons contact to turn it on.

For a step-by-step setup, read the guide [Getting the most out of KPI Cards](/en/blog/kpi-cards).
