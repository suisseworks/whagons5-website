Hotels run on work that repeats: rounds before arrivals, pool checks, the generator test on Tuesdays. **Work Plans** is the Whagons powerup for those routines. You define once what gets done, where, by whom and how often, and Whagons creates the tasks when they are due.

## What's new

- **Plans live in the team's space.** A plan is created from the Plans tab of the space that does the work, such as Maintenance, so its tasks land in the grid the team already uses.
- **One schedule per task and frequency.** Each schedule keeps the name, template, priority, tags, assignees, form, SLA and spots, and repeats daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.
- **Spots instead of copies.** Select every room, floor or area the task covers and Whagons creates one task per spot on every run. The screen tells you how many before you save, for example "Will generate 4 tasks per run".
- **You choose when tasks appear:** at the scheduled time, a set number of minutes before, or at the start of the scheduled day so the morning shift already has them.
- **Create task now** adds an extra run, like a check after a storm, without touching the recurrence.

## Check that it happened

The plan's **Calendar** shows each schedule as a row and each day as a column, by day, week, two weeks or month. A green dot is a completed day and a red triangle marks a late day. The **Runs** view lists the tasks each run created, for questions like which task went out for room 204 on Tuesday and how it ended.

## Availability

Work Plans is a powerup: it has to be active in your organization. The Admin, Leader and Member roles have access by default. If you don't see the Plans tab in your spaces, ask your admin or your Whagons contact.

For a step-by-step setup, read the guide [Getting the most out of Work Plans](/en/blog/work-plans).
