Underground utility services

Boring, trenching, excavation, locating and restoration. One contractor is responsible for the schedule from the first locate to the final patch.

  • Horizontal directional drilling

    Steerable, trenchless installation of conduit, pipe and cable under roads, landscape and waterways, with minimal surface disruption.

  • Open trench construction

    Trenching for large-diameter pipe and multi-conduit duct banks, by trencher, backhoe or hydro-vac.

  • Hydro-vacuum excavation

    Non-destructive excavation that exposes buried utilities safely and cuts restoration costs, even in tight spaces.

  • Utility service locating

    Private locating and pothole verification to clear conflicts before a single shovel hits the ground.

  • Turnkey utility projects

    Permitting, scheduling, construction and cleanup under one contract, all self-performed by our own crews.
