Hydro-Vacuum Excavation

Non-destructive excavation that exposes buried utilities safely and cuts restoration costs, even in tight spaces.

Request a Bid (951) 603-0745

Dig without damage

Hydro-vacuum excavation uses pressurized water or air to break up soil and debris, which is then safely extracted by vacuum. It exposes buried utilities with far less risk of damage than mechanical digging, and it reduces restoration costs.

Built for tight access

When space is restricted, hydro excavation is often the best way to get the job done. Horizon’s fleet includes multiple machine sizes to fit your project, and our operators have the experience for projects of any size.

Common applications

  • Potholing and daylighting
  • Excavation and trenching
  • Caissons
  • Utility locates to identify and protect existing lines
  • Sign posts and bases
  • Tunneling debris removal
  • Excavating inside buildings
  • Excavating frozen ground, down to -20 degrees
