Open trench construction

Open cut trenching for large-diameter pipe and multi-conduit duct banks, plus saw-cutting and micro-trenching.

Request a bidCall (951) 603-0745

When open cut is the right method

Open trench is more disruptive than boring, but for larger-diameter pipe or many conduits and cables in one run it is often the better method.

Equipment and crews

Horizon has the equipment and operators for trenching jobs of any size, by trencher, backhoe or hydro-vac. When the trench is backfilled, our own concrete and asphalt crews restore the surface.

Related work we self-perform

  • Saw-cutting
  • Micro-trenching
  • Utility vault construction
  • Conduit rodding, verification and documentation
  • Concrete construction and asphalt paving
  • ADA-compliant paving and concrete
