Open trench construction

Trenching for large-diameter pipe and multi-conduit duct banks, by trencher, backhoe or hydro-vac.

Request a bidCall (951) 603-0745

When the job calls for an open cut

Open cut trenching is more invasive than boring, but it’s still a necessary method of conduit placement and utility installation. Larger-diameter pipe, or many conduits and cables in a single run, often call for an open trench.

The right equipment for any size trench

Our operators are skilled and experienced in most utility installation situations. Horizon has the equipment and people to complete trenching jobs of any size, by trencher, backhoe or hydro-vac. We restore the surface with our own concrete and asphalt crews.
