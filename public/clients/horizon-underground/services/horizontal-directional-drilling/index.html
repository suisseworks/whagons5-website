Service 01

Horizontal Directional Drilling

Steerable, trenchless installation of conduit, pipe and cable under roads, landscape and waterways, with minimal surface disruption.

Request a bid (951) 603-0745

Trenchless installation with minimal surface impact

Directional boring is a steerable, trenchless method of installing pipe, conduit or cable along a planned bore path, using a surface-launched drill rig. Because there’s no open trench, it’s the method of choice when excavation isn’t practical: under roads and driveways, through finished landscape and beneath waterways. It works in a wide range of soil conditions.

Where we started, and what we still specialize in

Horizon Underground began as a directional drilling contractor, and drilling is still the core of what we do. Our drill crews and fleet of rigs are ready for any directional drilling job.

Because we also self-perform the work around the bore, you get one accountable contractor from first locate to final restoration:

  • Before the bore: utility locating and hydro-vac potholing.
  • After it: conduit rodding, verification and documentation.
  • At the pits: concrete and asphalt restoration.

How we work

From first locate to final restoration.

  1. 01

    Plan & permit

    Route planning, estimating and permit acquisition, handled in-house by the team that will build it.

  2. 02

    Locate & pothole

    Existing utilities are located electronically, then exposed by hydro-vac potholing so the bore path is verified, not assumed.

  3. 03

    Pilot bore & ream

    The rig steers a pilot bore along the planned path, then reams it to size for the product going in.

  4. 04

    Pullback

    Conduit, pipe or inner-duct is pulled back through the finished bore in one continuous run.

  5. 05

    Verify & restore

    Conduit is rodded, verified and documented, and our own crews restore concrete and asphalt at the pits.

On the jobsite

FAQ

Common questions.

When is directional drilling a better choice than trenching?

When trenching isn't practical. Road and driveway crossings, finished landscape, waterways, and anywhere surface disruption and restoration costs need to stay low. For large-diameter pipe or many conduits in one run, open trench can be the better call. We do both, so we'll recommend the method that fits the job.

Do you work as the prime contractor or as a subcontractor?

Both. We contract directly with owners and utilities, and we work as a subcontractor to general contractors and engineering firms.

Where do you work?

We're based in Norco, in the Inland Empire, and licensed in California, Arizona and Nevada. Our crews have installed hundreds of miles of conduit and cable across the Western United States.

Can you handle permitting and planning?

Yes. Route planning, estimating, permit acquisition, project management and scheduling are all done in-house.
