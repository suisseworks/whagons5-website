The whole job under one contract

Horizon Underground can self-perform every part of an underground utility project. On a turnkey contract we are responsible for the full scope, from route planning and permits through construction, restoration and cleanup. You deal with one contractor and one schedule, and nothing is handed off to another sub.

Our own crews and equipment

We have been in underground construction for more than 25 years and have installed hundreds of miles of conduit and cable across the Western United States. Our project managers plan and schedule the work in-house, and our own crews and equipment build it.

How a turnkey job runs

  1. Our project managers handle route planning, estimating, permits and the schedule.

  2. We locate the existing utilities and pothole them with hydro-vac to confirm what is in the ground.

  3. Open trench along the route, and short directional bores under roads and driveways where trenching is not an option.

  4. We set vaults, place inner-duct and pull fiber, copper or coax, then rod, verify and document the conduit.

  5. Our concrete and asphalt crews restore sidewalks, ramps and pavement, and we clean up and close out the job.

Common questions

Can you take on only part of a project?

Yes. Every service we offer is available on its own, and we often work as a subcontractor on a single scope.

Do you work as the prime contractor?

Yes. We contract directly with utilities, carriers and owners, and we also work as a subcontractor to general contractors and engineering firms.

Who handles permits and scheduling?

We do. Route planning, estimating, permit acquisition, project management and scheduling are all done in-house.

Where do you work?

Our office and yard are in Norco, in the Inland Empire. We are licensed in California, Arizona and Nevada.
