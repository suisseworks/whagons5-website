Turnkey Utility Projects

Permitting, scheduling, construction and cleanup under one contract, all self-performed by our own crews.

One contractor, start to finish

Horizon Underground is a full-service underground utility infrastructure contractor. We can self-perform every aspect of underground utility construction, so your project moves without hand-offs between subs.

Experience you can count on

Our staff has the experience to complete your project as efficiently as possible: from permitting to scheduling, through construction and cleanup. We work as the prime contractor or as a subcontractor.

One contract covers

  1. 01Route planning & estimation
  2. 02Permit acquisition
  3. 03Project management, planning & scheduling
  4. 04Boring, trenching & hydro-vac excavation
  5. 05Utility vaults & substructure
  6. 06Inner-duct & cable installation
  7. 07Concrete, asphalt & ADA restoration
  8. 08Cleanup & closeout
