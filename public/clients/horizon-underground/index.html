About Horizon Underground

Horizon Underground is a full-service underground utility contractor based in Norco, California. We have been in underground construction for more than 25 years and have installed hundreds of miles of conduit and cable across the Western United States.

We self-perform every part of an underground utility project, so the whole job can sit under one contract. We work as the prime contractor or as a subcontractor, with our own crews, equipment and project managers.

What a turnkey contract covers

On a turnkey project we are responsible for the whole scope and the schedule. Any of this work is also available as a separate contract.

Before construction

  • Route planning and estimating
  • Permit acquisition
  • Project management and scheduling
  • Utility locating and potholing

Construction

  • Directional boring
  • Open trench and micro-trenching
  • Saw-cutting
  • Hydro-vac excavation
  • Utility vaults and substructure
  • Inner-duct and cable: fiber, copper, coax
  • Conduit rodding, verification and documentation

Restoration

  • Concrete construction
  • Asphalt paving
  • ADA-compliant concrete and paving
  • Cleanup and closeout

Who we work for

We contract directly with utilities, carriers, owners and agencies, and we work as a subcontractor to general contractors and engineering firms.

  • Telecom and fiber

    Conduit, inner-duct, and fiber, copper and coax cable for carriers and their contractors.

  • Wireless

    Underground power and fiber feeds to wireless sites.

  • Solar

    Trenching, boring and conduit for solar projects and their interconnections.

  • Electric

    Conduit, vaults and substructure for electric utilities and private owners.

  • Gas

    Directional bores for gas lines, and hydro-vac excavation around live gas facilities.

  • Railroad

    Trenchless crossings and utility work along rail corridors.

  • Public works

    Prevailing-wage projects for cities, counties and agencies. We are DIR registered and handle certified payroll.

Our number one priority is for every one of our employees to return home safely every day.

Horizon runs its own safety program, with trained safety coordinators on staff. We also work with GotSafety, a safety management consultant, for training and support.
