A drilling company that grew into the whole job.

Horizon began as a directional drilling contractor. Over more than 25 years in telecom and utility construction, we've grown into a full-service underground contractor. We locate, bore, trench, build vaults, pull cable and restore the street ourselves.

We work for carriers, utilities, solar developers, rail and public agencies, as the prime contractor or as a subcontractor, and we've installed hundreds of miles of conduit and cable across the Western United States.

Five ways we get a line in the ground.

See all services

  • Horizontal directional drilling

    Steerable, trenchless installation of conduit, pipe and cable under roads, landscape and waterways, with minimal surface disruption.

  • Open trench construction

    Trenching for large-diameter pipe and multi-conduit duct banks, by trencher, backhoe or hydro-vac.

  • Hydro-vacuum excavation

    Non-destructive excavation that exposes buried utilities safely and cuts restoration costs, even in tight spaces.

  • Utility service locating

    Private locating and pothole verification to clear conflicts before a single shovel hits the ground.

  • Turnkey utility projects

    Permitting, scheduling, construction and cleanup under one contract, all self-performed by our own crews.

What we put in the ground.

Before anyone digs, locators paint every buried line on the street in its APWA color, with a letter for what it carries. These are the lines we install, and this site uses the same colors.

We also bore under rail corridors and work for public agencies.

  • Communications

    Conduit, inner-duct, fiber, copper and coax for telecom carriers and wireless sites.

  • Electric

    Power conduit, vaults and substructure for electric utilities and solar projects.

  • Gas

    Directional bores for gas lines, and hydro-vac excavation to expose live lines safely.

  • Water

    Water line installation by directional bore or open trench.

  • Sewer

    Sewer line installation by directional bore or open trench.

Our number one priority is for every one of our employees to return home safely every day.

Safety doesn't happen by accident. It comes from a few routines we keep without exception, and from working with GotSafety, a safety management consultant, for ongoing education and support.

Horizon Underground safety routines
How oftenWhat happens
DailyA tailgate meeting before each shift to walk through the work and its hazards.
On every siteWorksite audits by our trained safety coordinators.
WeeklyA safety meeting on topics specific to the work underway.
