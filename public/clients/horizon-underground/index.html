03How a bore works

Under the road, not through it.

Directional drilling installs conduit and pipe beneath roads, landscape and waterways with minimal surface disruption. It's where Horizon started, and still the core of what we do.

  1. 01

    Plan & permit

    Route planning, estimating and permit acquisition, handled in-house by the team that will build it.

  2. 02

    Locate & pothole

    Existing utilities are located electronically, then exposed by hydro-vac potholing so the bore path is verified, not assumed.

  3. 03

    Pilot bore & ream

    The rig steers a pilot bore along the planned path, then reams it to size for the product going in.

  4. 04

    Pullback

    Conduit, pipe or inner-duct is pulled back through the finished bore in one continuous run.

  5. 05

    Verify & restore

    Conduit is rodded, verified and documented, and our own crews restore concrete and asphalt at the pits.

05Safety

“Our number one priority is for every one of our employees to return home safely every day.”

Safety doesn't happen by accident. Our program runs on four routines, and we work with GotSafety, a safety management consultant, for ongoing education and support.

  • Daily

    Tailgate meetings

    Every shift starts by walking through the day's work and its hazards.

  • On site

    Worksite audits

    Conducted in the field by our trained safety coordinators.

  • Weekly
