About Horizon

Drillers first. Full-service utility contractor today.

Horizon began as a directional drilling contractor. Over 25 years in the fast-paced telecommunications and utility construction industry, we've grown into a full-service underground infrastructure contractor for telecom, wireless, solar, electric, gas and railroad clients.

Our people are the lifeblood of the company. We hire trained, experienced crews and take pride in delivering excellent service and quality workmanship, as the prime contractor or as a subcontractor.

Safety

Everyone goes home safe. Every day.

Safety doesn't happen by accident. We've built a culture and a safety management program around communicating hazards early and practicing safe work every shift. We also work with GotSafety, a safety management consultant, for ongoing education and support.

  • Daily tailgate meetings

    Every shift starts by walking through the day's work and the hazards that come with it.

  • Worksite audits

    Conducted in the field by our trained safety coordinators.

  • Weekly safety meetings

    Topics specific to the work our crews are doing right now.

  • Quarterly all-hands reviews

    Every incident reviewed company-wide, plus additional safety training.

“Our number one priority is for every one of our employees to return home safely every day.”
