Service

Drafting

Experienced CAD operators support engineering design, permit applications, procurement packages and offshore and onshore construction and fabrication. Every drawing is independently reviewed by the project lead, project engineer and drafting department. Our drafting department brings over 25 years of construction drawing experience.

  • Design drawings Conceptual, FEED and DE — alignments and profiles, field layouts, riser and subsea tie-in assemblies, P&IDs and crossing details.
  • Permitting submittals Safety flow schematics, riser and tie-in drawings and crossing details for ROW and abandonment permits.
  • Fabrication drawings PIG launchers/receivers, PLEMs and skids, riser clamps, gusset plates, padeyes, reaction collars and more.
  • Area maps 2-D and 3-D conceptual maps from survey data, evolving into field layouts.
  • Construction drawings Rigging, vessel and topside deck layouts, survey route maps and isometrics.

Also proficient in Blue Marble Global Mapper (3-D mapping and contours) and CORPSCON (coordinate conversion).

Full life cycle

Concept to commissioning — and beyond.

Once a solution is selected, CTI can perform all the planning, permitting, procurement, contracting, inspection and management needed to complete it — anywhere in the world.

  1. Concept

    Due diligence, feasibility, route selection and AACE-class estimates.

  2. FEED

    Basis of design, technical specifications, survey and metocean coordination.

  3. Detailed engineering

    Wall thickness, OBS, cathodic protection, span, expansion and buckling.

  4. Permitting & procurement

    Local, state and federal permits; RFQs, bid evaluation, expediting, logistics.

  5. Fabrication & SIT

    Fabrication inspection, FAT and system integration testing.

  6. Installation

    Pipelay, SCR and riser installation, tie-ins and offshore construction management.

  7. Commissioning

    Fill, hydrotest, dewater and pre-commissioning; final job book.

  8. Decommissioning

    Asset retirement obligation assessment and decommissioning.

Health, safety & environment

Zero incidents.
Since day one.

8,000+ days without incident or injury since opening our doors in 2004.

Our corporate vision is exceeding expectations — and that vision is the foundation of our HSE culture. It starts with sound engineering for robust designs, continues through inspection of materials and workmanship, and carries into the field, where every individual is accountable for the safety of their work.

  • Safety first, every meeting

    Every kick-off and internal meeting tables a safety topic for our people, colleagues and clients.

  • Protected on every site

    Personal protective equipment is provided to all personnel entering a work site.

  • Anyone can stop the work

    Every team member is authorized to stop work over a safety or environmental concern — and stopping work never results in disciplinary action.
