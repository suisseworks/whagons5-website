Onshore Pipelines

Natural gas, industrial gas and liquid pipelines where terrain, crossings, permitting or the product make the job hard, delivered by a right-sized team or as a turnkey package.

Talk to an engineer See capabilities

Right-sized for the task at hand.

Whether the task is a small encroachment project, a quick estimate, or the routing and design of a 500-mile pipeline system, we size the team to the work. With our survey, right-of-way and environmental associates, we can deliver a turnkey project package.

Our offshore depth carries straight over. The stress analysis tools we use every day for offshore facilities also review pump and compressor station piping, process piping and other onshore pipe under unusual stress.

  • Liquid ammonia, including pipe-in-pipe
  • Natural gas transmission and gathering

  • Acetylene and nitrogen
  • Refined petroleum products

Technical capabilities.

Open any item for a short description of the work.

Due diligence studies

Integrity and capability reviews of brownfield and greenfield assets before acquisition or investment, from gathering systems and loading facilities to transmission lines, on assets valued from hundreds of thousands to more than three billion dollars.

Feasibility studies

Net present value studies built on the route, the line-size hydraulics and the total installed cost.

Route selection

Routes optimized for the combined cost and schedule of constructability, right-of-way and environmental and permitting issues.

Cost estimating

Estimates to every AACE class, from quick order-of-magnitude figures to crew-up and unit-cost estimates and replacement costs.

Hydraulic studies

Compressible and incompressible flow studies in PIPEPHASE and WinFlow.

Stress analysis

Abaqus and CAESAR II analysis of pump and compressor station piping, process piping and other highly stressed pipe.

Permits and right-of-way

Railroad, highway, levee and waterbody crossing permits secured in-house, local, state and federal permits, and environmental permits with our associates. We also help secure permanent easements and temporary workspace.

Code compliance audits and evaluations

Mock audits and code reviews that prepare you for review by the regulator.

Horizontal directional drilling (HDD)

Strength and fatigue evaluation of pipe installed by HDD, for new crossings and replacements.

Construction management and inspection

Construction managers, fabrication coordinators and inspectors for pipelines and their associated facilities.
