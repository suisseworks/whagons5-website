Advanced Analysis: FEA & CFD

When conventional design methods are too conservative, or can't answer the question at all, our PhD-level analysts build the finite-element and CFD models that can.

Talk to an engineer See capabilities

Precision where it pays.

In today's subsea developments, conventional design methods are often too conservative, or simply inadequate. That is where finite-element analysis earns its keep: it can remove mitigation you don't need, or prove the design you have.

Our analysts have extensive experience in numerical simulation with state-of-the-art software, and work alongside our MSc-level pipeline engineers and licensed Professional Engineers. The same depth backs our Integrity Management work, re-qualifying existing assets for extended duty.

Global models

  • Full-scale models of an entire structure
  • Pipeline installation, buckling and free spans
  • Riser and umbilical configurations
  • I-tube pull-in structural integrity
  • Interaction with soils and fluids

Local models

  • Non-linear material, geometry and boundary conditions
  • Hot-spot stress and stress concentrations
  • Local buckling and local yielding
  • Welded joints and fatigue-critical details

Through every phase of the project.

  1. Design

    • Replace conservative hand checks with analysis
    • Avoid unneeded wall thickness or mitigation
    • Find the load case that actually governs

  2. Installation

    • Pull-in, lift and lay analyses
    • Independent verification of contractor analysis
    • Repair system simulation before deployment

  3. Operation

    • Fatigue life assessment
    • Strength assessment for extended duty
    • Re-qualification for a change of use

Technical capabilities.

Open any item for a short description of the work.

Global buckling and walking

Progressive, standards-based lateral buckling assessment of high-temperature, high-pressure pipelines, from screening to refined analysis on the real 3D seabed.

Thermo-mechanical qualification

Full-route qualification for local buckling, fatigue, end expansion, buoyancy loss and walking, from one consistent global model.

Free span and VIV fatigue

Allowable span lengths, span screening and interacting-span checks, with mitigation design for the spans that need it.

Rigid jumper strength, fatigue and clash

Strength, fatigue from VIV, slugging and thermal cycles, connector loads and clash checks for M- and Z-shaped jumpers.

Fatigue analysis

Cyclic loads from hydrodynamic forces, vibration and thermal expansion, turned into predicted fatigue damage for each member and welded joint over the service life.

Pipe-soil interaction

Soil resistance models, at low, best and high estimates, that carry embedment forward from one load step to the next.

Installation analysis

Global models of pipelay, riser and umbilical pull-in and subsea lifts, giving the loads on the pipe, the vessel and the equipment.

Flow-induced vibration and CFD

Computational fluid dynamics for hydrodynamic loads and flow-induced vibration, including vortex-induced vibration of spans, risers and jumpers.

Seismic assessment

Seismic response of jumpers, spools and subsea structures to ISO 19901-2.
