Design & Drafting

Construction-ready drawings in 2D and 3D, to your CAD standards, each one reviewed independently three times before it reaches you.

Talk to an engineer See capabilities

Drawings you can build from.

Our designers support engineering design, permit applications, procurement packages and every kind of offshore and onshore construction and fabrication. The drafting department brings more than 25 years of construction drawing experience.

Every drawing goes through a three-part QA/QC review: the project lead, the project engineer and the drafting department each check it independently.

Technical capabilities.

Open any item for a short description of the work.

Design drawings

Conceptual, FEED and detailed design drawings: pipeline alignment sheets and profiles, field layouts, riser assemblies, subsea tie-in assemblies, P&ID schematics, crossing details and subsea equipment details.

Permitting submittals

Safety flow schematics, riser and tie-in drawings and crossing details for right-of-way and abandonment permits.

Fabrication drawings

Custom equipment including pig launchers and receivers, PLETs and skids, riser clamps, gusset plates, padeyes, reaction collars and miscellaneous topsides and subsea equipment.

Area maps

Conceptual area maps in 2D and 3D from survey data, which become the field layouts in later phases.

Construction drawings

Rigging drawings for custom equipment, vessel and topsides deck layouts for equipment spreads, survey route maps and isometrics of subsea equipment.
