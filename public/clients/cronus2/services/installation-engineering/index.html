Installation Engineering

Offshore work is high-risk and high-cost. Proactive installation engineering, from procedure development through start-up, keeps the campaign safe and on schedule.

Talk to an engineer See capabilities

Engineered before it leaves the dock.

Cronus provides installation engineering from procedure development through start-up. We model what the pipe, the structure, the vessel and the equipment will see, so there are no surprises offshore.

Whether it is a preliminary static or dynamic analysis early in a project, or a detailed review and verification of the installation contractor's own analysis, we can support every stage of the campaign.

Typical deliverables

  • Installation analysis reports
  • Lift, rigging and overboarding plans
  • Sea-fastening designs and vessel deck layouts
  • Installation procedures
  • Independent verification of contractor analysis

Technical capabilities.

Open any item for a short description of the work.

Riser and umbilical pull-in analysis

Installation analyses for steel and flexible risers and umbilicals, including steel lazy-wave riser hand-over. Global finite-element models give the loads and stresses in the riser and the loads on the vessel and pull-in equipment.

Lifting and overboarding

Lift and overboard plans for tie-in spools, jumpers and other subsea equipment, with the analysis that keeps the asset and the crew safe during the operation.

Sea fastening and transportation

Vessel layouts and sea-fastening designs that keep cargo stowed and undamaged in transit, following industry practice and backed by analysis.

Pipelay installation analysis

Static and dynamic S-lay and J-lay analyses, from early-stage studies to detailed review of the installation contractor's analysis.

Subsea pipeline repair

Analysis for shallow-water and deepwater pipeline repair systems. Global models simulate system integration testing (SIT) and the repair itself, to verify performance before deployment.

Installation procedures

Step-by-step installation procedures for subsea equipment, including well-containment hardware.

Platform-based lifting operations

Engineering and project management for platform-based lifts, such as riser flex joint replacements.
