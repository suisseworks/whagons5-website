Subsea & SURF Engineering

Subsea umbilicals, risers and flowlines (SURF), designed, procured and installed for deepwater tiebacks and field developments, and proven in nearly 9,000 ft of water.

Talk to an engineer See capabilities

From the tree to the host.

Cronus supports subsea developments from FEED through detailed design and execution: flowlines and risers, rigid jumpers and tie-in spools, PLETs and PLEMs, and the package management of flexible pipe, umbilicals and subsea hardware, including trees, manifolds and controls.

We stay with the work through fabrication, factory acceptance and system integration testing (FAT and SIT) and offshore installation, so the design intent carries all the way to the seabed.

Systems we work on

  • Deepwater tiebacks with flexible flowlines and rigid insulated jumpers
  • Pipe-in-pipe flowlines with PLEMs at each end
  • Steel catenary risers (SCRs) and flex joints
  • Export pipelines with in-line wye assemblies
  • Gooseneck and bend restrictor assemblies
  • Pipeline repair systems for water depths to 10,000 ft

Through every phase of the project.

  1. FEED

    • Field layout and system architecture
    • Basis of design and specifications
    • RFQ packages for long-lead equipment

  2. Detailed design

    • Jumper and spool design and analysis
    • Gooseneck and bend restrictor verification
    • ROV access and clash checks

  3. Procurement

    • Package management: flexibles, umbilicals, trees, manifolds and controls
    • Vendor document review
    • Expediting

  4. Fabrication & testing

    • Fabrication inspection
    • FAT and SIT procedure review and witnessing
    • Jumper fabrication and testing

  5. Installation

    • Installation analysis and procedures
    • Offshore engineering and construction support
    • Pre-commissioning: fill, hydrotest and dewater

Technical capabilities.

Open any item for a short description of the work.

Rigid jumper design

Strength, fatigue, connector loads and clash for M- and Z-shaped jumpers, qualified to a standards-based methodology.

Tie-in spool design

Spool geometry, flexibility and stress, from the metrology through to installation.

PLET, PLEM and in-line structures

Pipeline end terminations, pipeline end manifolds and in-line wye assemblies, designed for installation and operating loads.

Steel catenary risers and flex joints

Riser design and analysis, and flex joint replacement on tension-leg platforms and spars, including the lifting, isolation and recommissioning work.

Goosenecks and bend restrictors

Gooseneck bend verification and installation analysis, hold-back anchor locations and bend restrictor global loads.

Flexible pipe and umbilical package management

Specifications, vendor selection and document review through manufacture, testing and load-out.

Subsea hardware package management

Trees, manifolds and controls, managed from RFQ through FAT and SIT.

Pipeline repair systems

Repair systems that replace damaged pipe sections up to 200 ft long, in water depths to 10,000 ft.

Brownfield tie-ins

Tiebacks into existing infrastructure, including topsides modifications of brownfield systems.
