Managed from feasibility to close-out.

Our project managers, construction managers, project engineers, HSE professionals, regulatory specialists and inspectors manage field development and construction for domestic and international projects.

They work with our engineers to develop solutions that are both technically sound and cost-effective. Once a solution is chosen, we can handle the planning, permitting, procurement, contracting, inspection and management needed to complete it, while cutting construction time and cost without compromising safety or quality.

What we manage.

Project management

  • A culture of safety and quality, championed from the top
  • Project execution plans
  • Schedule and cost management
  • Risk identification and management
  • Procurement and contracting strategies
  • Contracts, purchase orders and subcontracts
  • Direction and supervision of project activities

Construction management

  • Tender documents and specifications
  • Construction procedure review
  • Pipeline installation supervision
  • FAT and SIT procedure review and on-site observation
  • Interface with installation contractors and third parties
  • Jumper, flying lead, spool, connector and riser installation
  • Pre-commissioning: fill, hydrotest and dewater
  • Final job book and records

Project controls

  • Budget, cost and man-hour reporting by deliverable
  • Earned value against agreed credit rules
  • Progress tracking and forecasting
  • Critical path and float variance
  • Document control and the master document register

Materials management

  • RFQ packages, bidder clarifications and bid evaluation
  • Recommendations to award
  • Procurement schedules and long-lead items
  • Expediting, transportation and traceability
  • Interface materials checked for fit and compatibility

Interface and risk management

  • Framing sessions that assign every responsibility
  • Interface meetings and an interface register
  • Risk sessions graded on cost, schedule, safety, environment and reputation
  • Risk matrix, mitigation plans and periodic reports

Quality and HSE

  • A documented Quality Policy and written procedures
  • Lessons learned and voice of the customer
  • Stop-work authority for everyone, with no penalty for using it
  • A safety topic at every kickoff and internal meeting
  • No incidents or injuries since we opened our doors in 2004
