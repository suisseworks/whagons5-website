Why engineers join, and stay.

  • Culture

    How it feels to come to work on a Monday.

    Three values and twelve behaviors set the standard for how we work, and everyone is coached against them. You’ll work inside client teams, in the field and on advanced analysis, on some of the most demanding pipelines in the industry.

  • Total rewards

    The full package, and a sensible work-life balance.

    • Competitive compensation and bonuses
    • 401(k) retirement plan
    • Medical, dental and vision insurance

  • Trajectory

    Where you could be at Cronus in five years.

    Engineers and analysts progress through defined grades, on a people-leadership track or a technical-specialist track. We recruit from graduate level, offer internships from freshman year, and give new joiners real responsibility early.
