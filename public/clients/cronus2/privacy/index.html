Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

  • Last updated October 23, 2026

This is a draft for Cronus to review and approve, with its own legal advisers where needed, before the site launches. It describes the site as built: a contact form, a careers application form, Google Analytics and Cloudflare.

Who we are

This website is run by Cronus Technology, LLC (“Cronus”, “we”, “us”), 333 Cypress Run, Suite 300, Houston, TX 77094, United States. If you have a question about this policy or your information, write to info@cronustec.com or call 713.787.9444.

What we collect

When you contact us. The contact form asks for your name, email address and message, and optionally your company, phone number and how you’d like to work with us. If you email or call us directly, we receive whatever you choose to send.

When you apply for a job. The application form asks for your name, email address and the discipline you’re interested in, and optionally your phone number, LinkedIn profile and a cover note. You attach your résumé, so we also receive whatever it contains.

When you browse the site. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, the type of device and browser, approximate location (country or city, from the IP address) and actions such as downloading a paper, sending a form or clicking a phone number or email address. Google Analytics uses cookies for this. Our site is delivered and protected by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and technical details of each request to keep the site fast and secure, and may check whether a form is being sent by a person rather than an automated program.

We don’t ask for sensitive information such as health or financial details, and please don’t include it in a message or résumé.

How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry and, if you become a client, to work with you.
  • To consider your application for a role at Cronus.
  • To run, secure and improve the website, and to understand which content is useful.
  • To meet legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations.

We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the site and our business, and only for that purpose:

  • Cloudflare, which delivers and protects the website.
  • Our website hosting provider.
  • Resend, which delivers the messages and applications sent through the site’s forms to our inbox.
  • Google, which provides Google Analytics.
  • Our email and office software providers.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and related correspondence for as long as we need them to respond and to keep normal business records. We keep job applications for up to two years so we can consider you for later openings, unless you ask us to delete yours sooner. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) to tell visits apart. Cloudflare may set a cookie needed to protect the site from automated traffic. We don’t use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on. For visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Analytics runs without cookies.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop using it. Write to info@cronustec.com and we’ll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or certain US states, you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Security and international visitors

The site is served over an encrypted connection, and we limit who can see form submissions. Cronus is based in the United States, so information you send us is processed and stored in the United States.

Children

This site is for business visitors and job seekers. It isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top of the page.
