Since March 2004, Cronus Technology, Inc. (CTI) has specialized in the entire life cycle of onshore and offshore pipelines and subsea developments, with specialists in floating and fixed-facility fabrication and installation.

Our success comes from a team that is passionate about exceeding client expectations. Through knowledgeable people, commercial software, proprietary standard worksheets and an extensive library of industry codes and standards, we deliver economical solutions without compromising on safety, environmental responsibility or technical rigor.

Shallow water pipeline

  • Permitting and installation of fairway crossings

Project & risk management

Our risk management professionals work in tandem with project teams to identify and assess risks. Impacts are graded on cost, schedule, personnel safety, environmental impact and public perception of the operating company. A risk matrix captures all high-to-medium risks, and we work collectively with the project team on robust, innovative mitigations, with periodic status reports to project management.

Drafting

AutoCAD® drafting, with every drawing reviewed by the project lead, the project engineer and the drafting department.

Drafting

Experienced CAD operators support engineering design, permit applications, procurement packages and offshore and onshore construction and fabrication. Every drawing is independently reviewed by the project lead, project engineer and drafting department. Our drafting department brings over 25 years of construction drawing experience.

  • Design drawings Conceptual, FEED and DE: alignments and profiles, field layouts, riser and subsea tie-in assemblies, P&IDs and crossing details.
  • Permitting submittals Safety flow schematics, riser and tie-in drawings and crossing details for ROW and abandonment permits.
  • Fabrication drawings PIG launchers/receivers, PLEMs and skids, riser clamps, gusset plates, padeyes, reaction collars and more.
  • Area maps 2-D and 3-D conceptual maps from survey data, evolving into field layouts.
  • Construction drawings Rigging, vessel and topside deck layouts, survey route maps and isometrics.

Also proficient in Blue Marble Global Mapper (3-D mapping and contours) and CORPSCON (coordinate conversion).

Concept to commissioning, and beyond.
