Design & Drafting

Alignment sheets, crossings, spools and construction drawings, in 2D and 3D, to client CAD standards.

Design & Drafting

Experienced designers support engineering design, permit applications, procurement packages and offshore and onshore construction and fabrication. Every drawing is independently reviewed by the project lead, the project engineer and the drafting department, which brings over 25 years of construction drawing experience.

  • Design drawings Conceptual, FEED and detailed design: alignment sheets and profiles, field layouts, riser and subsea tie-in assemblies, P&IDs and crossing details.
  • Permitting submittals Safety flow schematics, riser and tie-in drawings and crossing details for right-of-way and abandonment permits.
  • Fabrication drawings Pig launchers and receivers, PLEMs and skids, riser clamps, gusset plates, padeyes, reaction collars and more.
  • Area maps 2D and 3D conceptual maps from survey data, evolving into field layouts.
  • Construction drawings Rigging, vessel and topside deck layouts, survey route maps and isometrics.

Drawn in Autodesk AutoCAD®, with Blue Marble Global Mapper for 3D mapping and contours and CORPSCON for coordinate conversion.

08About

Proud servants of the pipeline industry.

Founded in Houston in 2004, Cronus has grown from embedding specialists and inspectors with pipeline operators into a full-service pipeline engineering and integrity partner, combining PhD-level analysis with hands-on project experience.

Mission
To ensure pipelines are born right, through design, engineering and construction support, and then live right, remaining fit for purpose through integrity management.
Vision
To achieve brand recognition as a top-tier player serving the pipeline community.

Our story

  1. 2004

    Cronus founded in Houston, providing Embedded Project Specialists to pipeline operators and On-site Inspectors at fabrication yards, aboard vessels and at pipelay locations.

  2. Late 2000s

    In-house engineering team established to deliver pipeline projects from Houston.

  3. 2010s

    PhD-level advanced analysis added alongside MSc-level pipeline engineers.

  4. 2024

    Integrity Management launched, applying our analysis depth to keep existing assets fit for purpose.

  5. 2025–26

    New ownership, conversion to Cronus Technology, LLC, and a refreshed brand: new energy in pipelines.

The mark

The Cronus mark is drawn from pipeline geometry. Its circle echoes the cross-section of a pipe and its S-curve the continuous flow a pipeline exists to carry, with the letters of the name traceable within the device. Cronus, the Titan of time, suits a business whose work is built to last for decades.
