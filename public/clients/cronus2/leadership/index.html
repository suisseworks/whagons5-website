Leadership

Engineers, analysts and project people who have spent their careers on pipelines, most of them at Cronus for well over a decade.

BR

Bill Rees

Chief Executive Officer

Bill Rees is the Chief Executive Officer of Cronus. He brings more than 30 years of pipeline industry experience across sales, operations, and general management, with a focus on pipeline integrity, inspection, and maintenance services.

Most recently he was Vice President, Western Hemisphere at T.D. Williamson, where he led a 750-person organization covering sales, manufacturing, and service operations across the Americas. Earlier roles include leading T.D. Williamson’s business in Central Europe, scaling PII Pipeline Solutions through its sale to GE, and founding a consulting firm that served pipeline operators in Latin America, North Africa, and Central Europe. He has presented on integrity management at international pipeline conferences.

Credentials

  • Bachelor of Planning (Distinction) and B.A. (Honors), Town Planning, University of Manchester

Greg Dodge

Director, Business Line, Integrity Management

Greg Dodge leads the Integrity Management business line at Cronus. He has more than 30 years of experience in offshore oil and gas, including 25 years on subsea projects with engineering and installation contractors and a flexible pipe manufacturer. He began his career in project cost control and scheduling on large engineering and construction projects.

He joined Cronus in 2007 as Engineering Manager and later became Director of Engineering. He directed the design and analysis methodologies behind many of the company’s core services, including high-pressure, high-temperature subsea tiebacks and deepwater pipelines crossing complex seafloor profiles. Earlier roles include designing flexible pipe, serving as installation engineer aboard an installation vessel, and managing engineering and equipment packages on deepwater West Africa tieback developments.

Credentials

  • B.S. Civil Engineering, University of South Alabama

Jim Gordon

Director, Business Line, In-house Projects, Embedded Project Specialists, On-site Inspectors

Jim Gordon leads Cronus’s In-house Projects, Embedded Project Specialists, and On-site Inspectors business lines. With more than 25 years in major deepwater capital projects, he has overseen design, drafting, regulatory compliance, contracting, procurement, fabrication, and installation of marine pipelines and facilities.

Early in his career he worked on Gulf of Mexico deepwater export pipelines and steel catenary risers, including as client representative during offshore installation. At Cronus since 2004, he was seconded to two major deepwater integrated project teams, where he developed contracting strategy, ran bid evaluations, and managed offshore installation campaigns, including the wet tow and mooring installation of a floating production unit.

Credentials

  • B.S. Civil Engineering, Texas A&M University

Diego Zuniga

Director, Marketing & Sales

Diego Zuniga leads project development, proposals, marketing, and key client relationships at Cronus. He joined the company in 2007 as a project engineer on deepwater export pipeline projects, working on route selection, hydraulics, on-bottom stability, and installation support.

He then spent several years embedded with an operator’s capital projects team as an engineering supervisor, leading front-end engineering and contracting packages for pipeline replacement projects. Since moving into project development in 2017, he has managed pre-FEED, FEED, and detailed engineering projects, onshore and offshore, and helped form Cronus’s onshore pipeline division. He is bilingual in English and Spanish and conversational in Portuguese.

Credentials

  • B.S. Industrial Engineering, Texas Tech University

Chandler Dabbs

Director, Operations, Pipeline Engineering

Chandler Dabbs leads Cronus’s pipeline engineering group. He has worked in subsea pipeline engineering at Cronus since 2007, progressing from engineering support to technical development supervisor and, in 2023, to leading the group.

He has led front-end and detailed engineering for export, gathering, and infield pipeline systems from shallow water to ultra-deepwater, covering wall thickness, on-bottom stability, thermo-mechanical buckling, free span, cathodic protection, pipe-soil interaction, rigid jumpers, risers, and tie-in spools. He has also supported operators during offshore installation, pipeline repair system development, and integrity management programs.

Credentials

  • B.S. Civil Engineering, The University of Texas at Austin

Eulalio Fernandez, Ph.D., P.E.

Director, Operations, Advanced Analysis

Eulalio Fernandez leads Cronus’s Advanced Analysis group. He holds a Ph.D. in civil engineering and is a licensed Professional Engineer in Texas and Louisiana, with more than 20 years of experience in the analysis and design of offshore and onshore energy infrastructure.

His work covers nonlinear and dynamic finite element analysis, fatigue, vortex-induced vibration, global buckling, pipe-soil interaction, and installation analysis for pipelines, risers, jumpers, and tie-in spools. He has served as engineer of record on carbon capture and high-pressure gas injection pipelines, and has developed machine-learning response surfaces to screen subsea jumper operating conditions for integrity management. He joined Cronus in 2012.

Credentials

  • Ph.D. and M.S. Civil Engineering, The University of Texas at Austin
  • B.S. Civil Engineering, Universidad Autónoma de Chihuahua
  • Professional Engineer (TX, LA)

Michelle Doucette

Manager, Commercial Operations

Michelle Doucette leads commercial operations at Cronus. She joined the company in 2013 as an administrative assistant and has since worked across project controls, proposals, contracts, and human resources, which gives her a full view of how Cronus projects are won, set up, tracked, and closed out.

She supervises the project controls and document control teams, which track man-hours, cost budgets, and project documentation, and she manages the administrative staff.

Credentials

  • B.S. Psychology, University of Houston
